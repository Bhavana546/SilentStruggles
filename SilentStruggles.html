If you're feeling overwhelmed right now, try this first:
🌬️ 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding: Name 5 things you can see. 4 you can touch. 3 you can hear. 2 you can smell. 1 you can taste. This brings your nervous system back to safety.
💧 Drink water slowly. Cold water activates the vagus nerve and can reduce a panic response within 60 seconds.
📱 Text someone you trust. You don't have to explain everything. "I'm having a hard time right now" is enough.
